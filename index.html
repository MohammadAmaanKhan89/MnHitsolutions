FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How long does a project take?

Logo designs are typically delivered within 3–5 business days. Website projects take 2–4 weeks depending on complexity and client feedback time.

What information do you need to get started?

We need your business name, industry, preferred colors or style, and any reference examples. A brief contact form submission is enough to get us started.

How many revisions are included?

All packages include unlimited free revisions within the revision window. We work until you are completely satisfied with the result.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, and credit/debit cards. All prices are listed and charged in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).

Can I request a refund?

Yes. Refund requests must be submitted within 5 days of initial delivery. Please see our Refund Policy for full details on eligibility and tiers.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes, we work with clients across Pakistan and internationally. All communication is handled via email, WhatsApp, or video call.

Will I own the final designs?

Yes. Upon project completion and full payment, all intellectual property rights for the final approved designs are transferred to you.
